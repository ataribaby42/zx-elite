 Another Elite thread, I know. Felt this deserved its own though.

 This took a few days to put together and some variables were a bit harder to find. A few wouldn't change anything by modifying the memory directly so loading from tape over and over was required. A lot were found by stepping through various routines.

 If you feel like generating your own galaxies, simply change $003C - $0041 with any 8 bit numbers of your choice. I highly recommend you enter the same numbers into $0042 - $0047 as well, this will place you at planet 1 in your new galaxy with the right coordinates. The planet's tech level and government will be wrong, but once you've made your first hyperspace jump everything will be corrected.

 There are a total of 4 'NULL' variables in the data, which aren't addressed directly by the code nor ever changes during gameplay. These are placed among the equipment so perhaps Torus considered adding even more items to the game at some point.

 For the equipment; anything but $00 counts as equipped.

 If 'Mission 1 Event Activated' is $00 and the 'Mission 1 trigger flag' is set then that means that Mission 1 is completed. Having the Cloaking Device in your possession acts as the Mission 2 completed flag. The 3rd mission has its own flag as seen in the data above.

 The last 9 bytes are the 'Player's security code' used for the competition back in the day. It's loaded into memory but never read by the program. When you save, the game calculates the 'security code' based on your progress and overwrites the unread data. Since the competition is long over and changing the 'security code' in the save data doesn't accomplish anything I decided not bothering to decipher it.

 Tomas is correct, the additional last two bytes always seems to be $00, $14 in the 128k version. The only other difference is that the 48k game pads the filename with spaces ($20) to fill the complete 10 bytes, while the 128k game pads the the bytes with the EOL marker ($00).
