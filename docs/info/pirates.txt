Elite - Ship Boarded by PIRATES!
ErshErshFebruary 2016 in Games  Quote Thanks
Yesterday I sat down behind the debugger and decided to work out what causes the "Ship Boarded by PIRATES!" event that can sometimes, very rarely, happen in the Spectrum version of Elite. I thought there might be something one could do to avoid it, but unfortunately it turns out it's random. However the likelihood of it happening differs depending on what type of government you're in.

0.39% likelihood in Corporate States and Democracies

0.78% likelihood in Confederacies and Communist States

1.17% likelihood in Dictatorships and Multi-Governments

1.56% likelihood in Feudal Worlds and Anarchies

The random number is constantly generated while flying but not when in menus nor while docked. The condition is checked and the event is triggered when you make a hyperspace jump. Once it has been triggered there's no way to avoid it, even if you jump to another system, when you dock it's game over. Also if you don't use the docking computer but fly manually to the station you'll notice that it's not rotating any more, very ominous.

Even if boarding is a small risk, if you're going to grind pirates for three-quarters of an hour, first make sure that the station is safe i.e. first dock then stay in the system while fighting and dock in the same station when you're done.

If you'd like to never be boarded by those pesky pirates, enter this poke:

poke 56907,254

On the other hand; if you'd like to see the red screen of doom, enter this poke and they will board your ship every time:

poke 56902,0

Not only do the pirates get a freebie, I've also noticed that the police seems to have some strange behaviour in ZX Elite.

Pretty much the only time I've ever seen the police here is when they attack you during your journey towards a planet. Perhaps the stations are better built here and they simply don't worry about a lone oddball chipping away at the paint.

In the 128k version there was at the end of a page and below a RET a few 0's with next entire page having the interrupt vector table so I stuck it in between there. I tested that it works, also flew around for awhile to see if anything overwrote the mod but it seems safe.
