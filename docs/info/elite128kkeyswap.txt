I did a quick key swap poke:
CODE: SELECT ALL

Nkeyswap
M 8 65384 4 1
M 8 65385 16 32
M 8 65432 1 4
Z 8 65433 32 16
Y
This will swap the B-key with the symbol-shift-key. Since the B-key was only used to re-center while looking at the map it shouldn't matter if it's symbol-shift now instead and a bit out of the way. (Also since they're swapped, the B-key will now instigate the save menu while docked.)

Haven't got a Plus nor 128k so I can say if it's any better, but on my PC keyboard it felt pretty good having increase/decrease speed closer to each other. Feel free to give it a go!
